# Implementation Roadmap — Auditable Multidirectional 5-Axis Slicer

This roadmap decomposes the features and initialization work stated in `PRD.md`, `architecture-design-revised.md`, `tech-stack-recommendation.md`, and `database/README.md` / `database/schema.sql`. Tasks are ordered by architectural dependency. Each task is intentionally sized for approximately one to two days of focused development or one isolated agent workspace.

The roadmap does **not** schedule capabilities explicitly excluded by the PRD, including automated support generation, continuous non-planar five-axis slicing, generalized inverse kinematics, calibrated pivot/tool-center-point compensation, adaptive layer height, arbitrary infill patterns, full swept-volume collision proof, and unattended physical printing.

| F-037 | Slice-plane value model | F-005 | Define plane origin, unit normal, angle pair, angle units, rotation order, local axes, and source-frame metadata. | Invalid normals, angle ranges, missing origins, and unsupported conventions are rejected. |
